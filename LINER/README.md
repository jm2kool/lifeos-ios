# LINER.

A personal, artwork-first Apple Music companion for iPhone (iOS 26), built from `SPEC.md`. Free-signed and sideloaded:
CI (`.github/workflows/liner.yml`) builds every push and publishes `LINER.ipa` as the **liner-latest** release.
Install it with Sideloadly using your Apple ID.

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| Library | Artists · Albums · Songs · Playlists with search and sort; one-song "albums" are treated as songs; editions of an album ("WILD", "WILD (Deluxe)") are grouped into one album led by the biggest edition (Mixtapes have their own tab); albums as a moving crate with haptic ticks; black-and-white artist photos from Deezer |
| Search | The whole Apple Music catalogue — artists, albums and singles, songs; play, queue or add them to your library — or switch to Your Library |
| Artist pages | The artist's photo in full colour down most of the screen, melting into a page painted in the photo's own colour, with their stylised name and wide Play / Shuffle buttons; Edit (paintbrush) → Choose Photo puts your own picture there instead (black and white in lists and pins, and the page takes its colour from it); then A Page like Apple Music's (latest release, Top Songs, Albums, Singles & EPs from the Apple Music catalogue via the iTunes Search API, streamed by Apple Music ID — through the Music app's player if LINER.'s own one refuses; singles Apple lists without songs are found by name) and LINER. stats (your history with them) |
| Colour pages | Album pages keep the cover still and full width, fading out by the title into one solid colour from the cover (deepened so everything reads), like the old Music app. Artist, Apple Music release, playlist and mixtape pages are painted edge to edge with the artwork's colours, which drift and blend behind everything like the Apple Card in Wallet and lean as you tilt the phone; on artist pages it's the colour of their photo (or their covers when there's no photo). Holds still with Reduce Motion (and in Low Power Mode if you ask) |
| Artist titles | iOS 27-style stylised names: the artist's logo (TheAudioDB) in white — collaborations like "Drake & PARTYNEXTDOOR" get both logos joined with an "&" when the duo has none of its own — or genre-matched lettering; pick a style per artist in Settings → Artist titles |
| Playback | Library songs via `applicationQueuePlayer` (iCloud and catalogue songs stream by Apple Music ID, with one automatic retry), imported files via AVPlayer, mixed in one queue; shuffle, repeat, Up Next |
| Player | Always full-screen artwork aimed at the subject, motion covers or Living Covers behind it, glass controls tinted by the art; swipe the mini player left or right for the next or previous song; opens by itself when you start a song (Settings → Listening); the screen stays on while it's open |
| Lock screen | Animated 3:4 / 1:1 artwork (motion cover or Living Cover); guaranteed for mixtapes, best effort for Apple Music songs |
| Stats | My LINER.: totals, ranked artists/albums/songs; artist and album pages with your history and ranks |
| Passports | Song Passport (hearts, moods, notes, connection, Song DNA, favourite moments, saved lyrics); Album Passport (hearts, personal ranking, history, cover) |
| Album titles | The album's own title lettering, lifted from its cover with on-device text recognition when it's clean enough (Settings → Covers → Album title logos); plain text otherwise |
| Studio | LINER. Studio (the brush on an album page, or Edit in Studio on any album): up to four covers per album (the original, photos or videos), with the library grid, album page, player and Lock Screen, and Home pin each using the one you pick; crop, or extend a picture's edges to fill the square; motion for still covers (Zoom, Drift, Light, Focus, Particles, Film); logo, font, lettering and cover layout; the page colour and the tracklist's colours; a live preview of each place |
| Coloured tracklists | Album tracklists take their colours from the cover like the old Music app, on the page's solid colour: song names and numbers in the cover's colours that read on the page, the song playing in its most vivid colour with moving bars, and the Play button in that colour too (Settings → Covers → Coloured tracklists, or per album in Studio) |
| Customise Album | One sheet for everything about an album (Customise on its page, or from any album's long-press menu or its Passport): text size for the tracklist and title with a live preview, logo, font and full-screen or square cover, Full / Saved / edition tracklist and Manage Editions, cover photo or motion cover, cover lettering on or off, and the Home pin, its look and its cover. Text sizes apply to every album, release, playlist and mixtape page and are in Settings → Album pages too |
| Editions & pins | Full Album (every song from Apple Music, the ones you don't have streamed) or Saved Album (only your library's songs) per album; your own versions of albums (library + mixtape tracks, custom covers); pins on Home in a grid that fits how many there are; playing an edition shows its cover in the player, the mini player and on the Lock Screen |
| Mixtapes | Import mp3/m4a/wav/aiff/mp4/mov from Files or "Open in LINER."; covers, motion covers, LRC lyrics |
| Lyrics | LRCLIB synced lyrics full screen with word-by-word karaoke fill in three looks (Sing-along over the cover's colours in motion, Lyric video, Behind the cover with the words behind the person on it, set in Settings or the lyrics' •••); word stamps (enhanced LRC) are used when LRCLIB or your .lrc has them, mixtapes can be timed by listening on the iPhone, and other songs get a syllable-based estimate (a badge says which); nudge the timing per song; background vocals and duets laid out on their own; the controls fade while you sing; the player's lyric line fills word by word and zooms open into the lyrics; it opens on the line being sung, the current and next line on the player, tap to seek, save lines and moments; live lyrics on the Lock Screen (Live Activity that follows you from song to song, lines slide up as they change, its own clock and a keep-alive adapted from Lyricar (silence at full mixer volume, a 10 s watchdog, a background-task hold while it restarts) so it doesn't freeze in the background, moving on with the music when one song runs into the next, dimming if a line is ever overdue, a Live lyrics log in Settings to see why if it does, and a Dynamic Island with a little record, a progress ring that moves by itself, and the lyric with time left when expanded) |
| Your own logos | Add a logo to any album, Apple Music release or artist (Customise Album → Style on albums, ••• → Style on releases, Edit Logo on an artist) from Photos or Files; its background can be taken away. Show it in its own colours, black and white, white, black, the cover's colour or anything from the colour wheel; drag, pinch and twist it into place on the cover, and it becomes the title in the bar at the top as you scroll. It's also on pins, the sleeve and the record |
| Album fonts | Customise Album → Style → Choose Font (••• → Style on releases): any font on the iPhone or a .ttf/.otf you import, with weight, letter spacing and capitals, used for the album's title, tracklist (song names and the artist under them), sleeve, the title in the player and the lyrics. Add your own logo and the album takes the font that looks most like its lettering (read on the iPhone, never replacing a font you chose); Match the Logo in the font list does it again. Tracklists only name the artist on songs with someone else on them |
| Covers | Full-screen or square cover per album and release (••• → Style); Apple Music releases open full screen like your albums; Other Editions of an album at the bottom of its page |
| Pins | Albums pin as their cover with their logo; Choose Pin Cover picks from the album's cover, your editions' covers or any edition Apple lists |
| Opening | A record with your most played cover spins up, the tonearm drops the needle, and the record shrinks into the full stop of LINER. |
| Sleep timer | ••• in the player: 15–90 minutes, the end of this song or the end of the queue; the music fades out slowly (Settings → Listening → Sound sets how long), then the volume goes back where it was. A moon with the time left sits on the player |
| Automix | Crossfades of 2–12 s. Your mixtapes and files really overlap (two decks on LINER.'s own audio engine); Apple Music songs fade out and in, since iOS never gives apps their sound |
| DJ Mix | ••• → DJ Mix on a playlist, album or mixtape: per song, trim the start and end, set its level, loop a section any number of times and set the handover into the next song. Seamless on your own files; Apple Music songs jump for trims and loops and can only be made quieter |
| Equaliser | Ten bands and twelve presets for what LINER. plays itself; for Apple Music it points to the Music app's EQ in iPhone Settings |
| Edit lyrics | The pencil in the lyrics: tap along as each line starts, nudge a line by a tenth of a second, shift them all, change words, add or remove lines, or time lyrics that came without timings. Your edits survive refreshes, go in backups, and share as an .lrc file |
| Passport cards | Share as a printed passport page: the cover, title, artist and album, your library rank ("#3 of 8,968 songs · Top 1%"), hearts, plays and minutes, a played-count stamp and machine-readable lines, on the cover's colour. Top Albums / Songs / Artists of all time from My LINER. (share button) |
| For You | Made for You mixes with their own covers (the mix's name on its lead album's colour, with a few of its covers fanned in the corner), built on the phone (Heavy Rotation, Daily Mixes by genre, Rediscover, Fresh Finds, My Essentials for your top artists), and Apple Music's own playlists: editors' picks from its New page on Home, and each artist's Essentials and other playlists on their Page, read from music.apple.com's public pages (may stop working if Apple changes them). Tap a shelf's title to fold it away; it stays folded |
| Recaps | In My LINER.: week / month / year from the day you install, story mode, Monday notification |
| Share | 9:16 still and animated cards to Instagram Stories, Photos or anywhere; share lyrics like Apple Music (tap Share in the lyrics, pick up to six lines in a row, and they go on the story card over the cover) |
| Mood playlists | On-device Apple Intelligence recipe + local scoring; Mood Mix without AI; save to Apple Music |
| Vinyl Mode | Double-tap Home to switch; Home becomes the record crate (with a rise-and-focus transition); the lyric being sung is printed round the record and travels round with it, each line drifting into the next; albums open as a gatefold sleeve (lifts out of the crate and opens from the spine: the cover full screen with title, numbers and songwriters, the tracklist a swipe away on a blur of the cover with the album's logo; side by side as squares in landscape); "Put it on" slides the record out and drops it onto a plain record on the turntable; double-tap the record button in the crate for the Listening Room (a black turntable and a shelf of your most played records, spines out: take one off, open the sleeve, drag the record onto the deck; the screen stays on); needle drop, 33⅓ rpm spin, scratch-scrub, Side B; Cover/Record switch in the player opens a turntable deck whose tonearm follows the song — move it to seek, set it down off the record to pause |
| Demo mode | For CI only: `LINER_DEMO=1` swaps in a demo library (Charli xcx, Drake, Doja Cat, Yeat and ADÉLA albums with made-up play counts and history), and `LINER_SCENE` picks what happens (splash, crate, gatefold, room, player, lyrics, share, stats, recap, library, island). Recordings go to the **liner-preview** release |
| Backups | Logos, fonts, layouts and pin covers are in the JSON backup too |
| Settings | Covers, Low Power, lyrics, sounds, notifications, artist titles, recalibrate Apple Music, rebuild stats, JSON backup, test data, spike lab |

Limits of free signing: no MusicKit (MediaPlayer only), re-sign every 7 days. The live lyrics widget extension uses one extra App ID.
See `PHASE0.md` for the device checks and `CLAUDE.md` for working on the code.

Parts of the live lyrics keep-alive are adapted from [Lyricar](https://github.com/noluyorAbi/lyricar) (Apache 2.0); see `NOTICE`.
